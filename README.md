# Electromyography

## Abstract
This project focuses on building, adapting and testing an amplifier circuit capable
of capturing surface electromyography signals. The goal was to build a system that
could detect the low voltage produced by muscle activity, condition the signal, and
deliver a usable output for further analysis. The circuit uses an amplifier for the
initial differential gain, followed by active filtering and a second gain stage to
improve clarity and reduce noise. Surface electrodes were placed on the forearm
to record muscle activation during simple contractions. The final circuit produced
a stable and recognizable EMG waveform with a clear separation between the
resting and active states.
